About Can-Do

Can-Do Remodeling is a father-and-son company founded in 1997 and grown into a family business in 2017, with roots in carpentry and a broad range of remodeling and restoration work.

Tim & Rusty DeVorse

Experience meets a Can-Do attitude.

Tim brings more than 30 years in the trades, including carpentry and kitchen installations. Rusty joined the family business in 2017 after a career in sales and property management and now works across remodeling, restoration, finish carpentry, painting and drywall.
